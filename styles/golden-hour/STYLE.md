# golden-hour

Warm, low sun and long shadows. Emotional and epic.

| | |
|---|---|
| Sky | indigo top fading to orange at the horizon |
| Light | low orange sun from the side, warm ambient |
| Camera | fov 48, slow orbits and push-ins, 2.39:1-style bars + vignette |
| Cuts | every 3-5 s |
| Music | piano or strings, 70-90 bpm |
| Good for | farewells, journeys, tributes, "last day" stories |

Avoid: slapstick pacing. Let shots breathe; hold the reaction.
