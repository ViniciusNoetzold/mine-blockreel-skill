# Aurora Express shot list

30 seconds at 24 fps, 1280 × 720. The lanterns trace the path across the canyon. The courier's red cape and amber glowstone separate the silhouette from the snow. See [Director](../../DIRECTOR.md) for the camera and energy conventions used here.

| Shot | Time | Camera | Action and intent | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0.00–2.80 | Low dolly, then rise and widen | The courier enters carrying the only warm light. The track leads into the canyon beneath the title. | Wind, single rail ping, soft pad. |
| 2 | 2.80–5.40 | Slow push, front three-quarter | Wide eyes find the signal across the bridge. Hold before the sprint. | Wind over the low score. |
| 3 | 5.40–8.00 | Canted tracking shot | Follow the first strides and keep empty frame ahead of the runner. | Footsteps and plucked pulse enter together. |
| 4 | 8.00–10.70 | Broadside wide with shallow drift | The bridge geometry makes the lightning strike and collapse legible as the courier clears the span. | Metal warning, thunder and debris. Drums enter on the hit. |
| 5 | 10.70–12.20 | Low tracking, reverse tilt | Two decking blocks break and the courier jumps the opening with the glowstone raised. | Falling debris and landing thud. |
| 6 | 12.20–15.30 | Elevated tracking | The smoke falls behind while the signal fills the frame ahead. | Footsteps resume beneath the run cue. |
| 7 | 15.30–17.50 | Medium push | The courier slows into the tower's pool of red light. | The run cue falls away. |
| 8 | 17.50–19.75 | Tight frontal portrait | A breath and a pause before committing to the signal. | Wind with the score held out. |
| 9 | 19.75–22.48 | Side medium push | The glowstone moves from hand to socket and a flare confirms contact. | Return of the rail chime. |
| 10 | 22.48–25.50 | Tilt from the socket to the tower | The signal lights climb the tower and a burst makes the activation unmistakable. | Rising chimes and major-key score. |
| 11 | 25.50–30.00 | Wide crane out | Dawn reveals the lit signal and the broken span in the same frame. Fade on the final title. | Warm cadence, then wind and score fade. |
